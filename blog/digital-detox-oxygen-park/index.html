A Digital Detox Guide: Finding Peace at Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park

July 10, 20266 min read

Why We Need to Unplug

In our hyper-connected world, we spend an average of 7 hours a day looking at screens. Our brains are constantly bombarded with notifications, emails, and social media updates. At Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park, we invite you to put your devices away and let your soul breathe in a 19-acre living sanctuary.

Step Into the Canopy

The journey begins the moment you step onto the bamboo-lined nature trails. Surrounded by over five species of bamboo and native trees, the air is remarkably fresh, high in oxygen, and soothing to the senses. Walking here is an active form of meditation—a concept known in Japan as Shinrin-yoku or forest bathing.

A Feast for the Senses

A day at the Oxygen Park is filled with immersive, analog experiences:

  • Guided Trails: Learn about carbon sequestration, biodiversity, and local flora from our expert tribal guides.
  • Traditional Ranbhaji Buffet: Enjoy a rustic lunch prepared with wild, seasonal forest vegetables that are both healthy and flavorful.
  • Warli Art Workshops: Sit under the shade of ancient trees and paint your own Warli canvas using natural pigments.
  • Tarpa Dance: End your day joining hands with the tribal community in a traditional circular dance around the Tarpa player.
"You enter the park with a cluttered mind, but you leave with the quiet rhythm of the forest beating in your heart."

Ready to unplug? Book your weekend slot today and experience the ultimate digital detox just 100 km from Mumbai and Thane.
