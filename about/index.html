ABOUT US

A Welcome to the Living Classroom at Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park

Welcome to a place where curiosity grows wild, professional teams find renewal, and every visitor connects directly with the heart of nature. Nestled in the scenic hills of Jawhar—just a 100 km journey from the urban rhythms of Mumbai, Thane, Nashik, and Vapi—Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park warmly invites educational institutions, corporate organizations, families, and individuals to step into a vibrant, 19-acre nature-based experiential destination.

Here, the air is purer, the silence speaks, and the rustle of the leaves carries the stories of a community that poured its heart into the soil. What was once a stretch of quiet terrain has been transformed into a dense, flourishing eco-reserve featuring pristine natural water bodies, hilltop nature trails, and five distinct species of bamboo—including rare varieties brought from Assam. More than just a park, Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park is a living sanctuary designed to awaken the senses, cultivate environmental awareness, honor tribal heritage, inspire responsible living, and foster deep team collaboration.

SOCIAL IMPACT & EMPOWERMENT

Beyond the Park: Village Development & Women's Leadership

Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park is not just an eco-reserve, but a seedbed for transforming local tribal livelihoods through rural enterprise and bamboo skill development.

Creating Livelihoods & Local Jobs

By setting up sustainable bamboo operations, we have curbed seasonal migration to cities, providing dignified, stable incomes to tribal families directly within their own villages.

Bamboo Handicrafts Skill Training

Artisans undergo rigorous training in crafting eco-friendly bamboo goods, transforming raw resources into beautiful utility and gifting products under Project Green Gold.

Empowering Women Entrepreneurs

We foster self-reliance and local leadership, guiding village women from daily wage labourers to proud entrepreneurs who handle logistics, production, and accounts.

Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog (VBUPCL)

We are proudly associated with VBUPCL, India's first 100% tribal-owned bamboo enterprise, managed by tribal women directors and supporting over 700 people across 23 villages.

THE WONDER PLANT

Why Choose Bamboo?

Explore the exceptional environmental, commercial, and ecological benefits of nature's most sustainable building block.

Renewable Resource

Bamboo is one of the fastest-growing plants in the world, making it a highly sustainable and renewable resource. It can be harvested regularly without depleting the ecosystem.

Eco-Friendly and Sustainable

Bamboo helps combat climate change by absorbing large amounts of carbon dioxide. It also requires less water compared to other crops, making it an environmentally friendly choice.

Versatility and Durability

Bamboo is incredibly versatile and can be used in a wide range of products, including furniture, flooring, textiles, and kitchenware. It is also highly durable, often stronger than wood.

Biodegradable and Non-Toxic

Bamboo products are biodegradable, reducing waste and environmental impact. Additionally, bamboo is naturally free from pesticides and harmful chemicals, making it a safe and non-toxic option for various uses.
