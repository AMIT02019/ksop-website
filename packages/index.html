The day when forest becomes their classroom

One day in our 19-acre bamboo park where children follow a treasure map, build a bamboo craft beside tribal artisans, paint it in Warli, and present what they made — learning by doing, not just watching.

• 19-acre Oxygen Park• The Bamboo Quest• Build-It Workshop• Warli Art• Organic Village Lunch• Folk Dance & Tarpa
Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park Tour • Video

What your students take away

Teamwork & problem-solving

They crack the quest together — reading clues, sharing roles, thinking on their feet.

Focus, screens away

A whole day of hands-on attention, with phones set aside and curiosity switched on.

They make something real

Build a bamboo product, paint it in Warli, present it — and carry it home with pride.

Culture & empathy

Time with the tribal artisans whose craft, songs and stories shape the day.

Care for the living world

See how barren land became a 19-acre bamboo forest — sustainability you can walk through.

Why schools trust this place

  • ◆

    Run by the artisans themselves. A tribal-owned enterprise: 253 artisan-shareholders, 80% women, with all five directors women.

  • ◆

    Grown from Project Green Gold (2019). A Keshav Srushti initiative that has trained 800+ people across 28 villages in Palghar.

  • ◆

    Recognised nationally. Featured in PM Narendra Modi's 96th 'Mann Ki Baat' and awarded the Rashtriya Baans Bhushan 2025 at IIT Bombay.

  • ◆

    A real forest, restored. 19 acres turned from barren land into a living ecosystem with five bamboo species.
