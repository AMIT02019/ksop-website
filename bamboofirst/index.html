OUR STORY

Bamboo First: Rooted in Tradition, Growing towards Empowerment

Welcome to Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited. At Bamboo First, we fuse tradition with innovation to create a green revolution led by the strength and spirit of tribal women.

Our journey is rooted in history yet forward-thinking. Bamboo, a symbol of resilience and positivity, has been integral to our heritage for centuries. In 2004, visionary leader Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee recognised its potential to transform lives and economies. We've been committed to realising that vision ever since our inception in 2019.

With the support of Keshav Srushti, we launched the Keshav Srushti Gram Vikas Yojana in 2017, empowering farmers through groundbreaking solutions like solar-powered irrigation. That same year, a pivotal change occurred when Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi reclassified bamboo as grass, allowing for its unrestricted harvesting—a significant shift from its previous classification as a tree during British times, which made bamboo harvesting punishable.

In 2019, Project Green Gold came to life. We trained tribal women from a village in Vikramgad taluka in the art of bamboo craftsmanship. One full year of dedication resulted in our first widely accepted product with mass appeal, the bamboo rakhi, launched in June 2020. Even during the challenges of the Covid-19 pandemic, our community delivered over 51,000 rakhis and expanded our product line to over 50 utility-based bamboo items across various categories like general gifting, corporate gifting, mementos, festival products, kitchen items, toys, etc. Our design capabilities were further validated with an international order of 500 auto rickshaws from the UK.

On Gudi Padwa in 2023, we officially became Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited and launched Bamboo First. Today, we are India’s first 100% tribal owned bamboo enterprise, with over 253 shareholder artisans. 80% of our shareholders are tribal women, showcasing our deep community roots.

At Bamboo First, we stand for three essential E's: Environment, Empowerment, and Employment. We are dedicated to nurturing the environment through sustainable bamboo practices, empowering tribal women by honing their skills and providing them with leadership opportunities, and creating meaningful employment that drives economic growth within our communities. This commitment to the three E's is not just part of our story—it is our story.

TRUSTEE’S LETTER

A Message from Shri Bimal Kedia

Dear Patrons,

It gives me immense pleasure & satisfaction while sharing with you the successful 5 years of socio-economic transformation led by Keshav Srushti through its bamboo-based skill & entrepreneurship development initiatives, specially designed to empower tribal women. It’s a journey of a social initiative ‘Project Green Gold’ paving the way for a unique social bamboo enterprise ‘Vikramgad Bamboo Udyog Producer Company Limited’ (VBUPCL).

The striking feature of this company is that it is probably the only company which is fully Owned & Managed by Tribal people (Mainly Women). The CEO is a Tribal Youth and all its Directors are Tribal Women. It has 253 tribal bamboo artisans as shareholders.

Through this company we aim to create sustainable opportunities for Tribal Communities with a special focus on empowering Tribal Women and at the same time prioritising the protection of Environment.

I personally invite you to witness this powerful journey of Empowerment at few of our villages and become part of this movement. Look forward to your association in making this movement stronger and more impactful.

Once again I would like to humbly admit – Your Support made it Possible.

Regards,
Bimal Kedia
