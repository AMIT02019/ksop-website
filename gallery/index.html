🌿 THE EXPERIENTIAL JOURNEY

The Journey: What You Will Experience & Learn

Visiting Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park is a completely immersive, hands-on adventure where you learn, bond, and transform by doing, not just observing. It is an invitation to reconnect with what truly matters.

🍃

Discovering Ecological Secrets

As you explore the dense canopy and hilltop nature trails within Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park, you will dive into the science of carbon sequestration, understand the critical role of bamboo ecology, and learn what it truly means to be a responsible steward of our planet.

Navigating the Land & Mindful Detoxing

You will put your screens away to let your soul breathe, stepping onto breathtaking panoramic viewpoints, using your observation skills to read the landscape, track natural markers, and experience a refreshing digital detox amidst the pristine forest surroundings of Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park.

Enjoying and Exploring Tribal Life

You will step directly into the heart of a working tribal community to explore their sustainable, rural lifestyle. This unique opportunity lets you interact face-to-face with the local community, discover how they live in beautiful harmony with nature, and gain an authentic, deeply moving understanding of India’s rich indigenous heritage.

Bringing Heritage to Life

Inside the dedicated tribal art & craft spaces at Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park, you won't just look at history—you will touch it with your own hands. You will watch real bamboo craft demonstrations and then paint your own traditional Warli art, discovering how the indigenous community pours their lives and stories into simple geometric shapes.

Strengthening Corporate Teams

Designed as a dynamic corporate retreat, teams will engage in collaborative wilderness navigation and hands-on structural building challenges. These activities break through office hierarchies, rebuild authentic human connections, test group dynamics, enhance leadership skills, and introduce teams to practical ESG and circular economy models in active motion.

Unwinding and Playing Naturally

Beyond the learning, you will experience the pure, simple joy of the outdoors at Keshav Srushti Oxygen Park—tasting authentic local tribal food, witnessing vibrant cultural shows, and exploring custom bamboo structures and play areas designed for active, open-air recreation.

WHAT YOU WILL EXPERIENCE

Awaken the Senses, Build Connections

Explore custom-curated programs designed to inspire discovery, strengthen bonds, and restore peace.

Adventure

The Bamboo Quest (Treasure Hunt)

Students step away from their screens and into the wild, dividing into small teams to navigate 19 lush acres of serene forest surroundings. Guided by physical maps, they share laughs and solve clues that route them past pristine natural water bodies and up hilltop nature trails, learning to trust one another while cracking real-world environmental challenges.

Plan This Experience
Ecology

Assam Bamboo & Ecological Study

A gentle, guided journey through the park’s canopy highlighting five distinct species of bamboo, including rare varieties brought from Assam. Students don't just study carbon sequestration and bamboo ecology from a textbook—they touch the towering stalks, breathe in the pure oxygen, and feel what it truly means to be a protective steward of nature.

Plan This Experience
Crafts

Hands-On Tribal Art & Craft Workshop

Hosted within specialized tribal art & craft spaces, this workshop connects children to the joy of raw creation. They witness incredible live bamboo craft demonstrations and then sit side-by-side with local artisans, pouring their own imagination into assembling custom bamboo items and practicing authentic Warli painting to bring ancient folklore to life.

Plan This Experience
Heritage

Rural Lifestyle & Community Interaction

A deeply humbling, face-to-face interaction with a vibrant, working tribal community. By listening to their stories and exploring their sustainable lifestyle, students gain a heartwarming window into India's rich indigenous heritage, discovering how communities thrive through sustainable development and shared empathy.

Plan This Experience
Recreation

Play, Performance, and Reflection

Children run free and laugh out loud through custom bamboo play areas & structures. The unforgettable day wraps up with the grounding rhythm of vibrant tribal dance & cultural shows and a shared, authentic local tribal food experience—leaving them with hearts rooted and memories that will last a lifetime.
